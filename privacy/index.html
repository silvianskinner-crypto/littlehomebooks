Privacy

Last updated: October 1, 2026.

What we collect

When you buy a book, the payment provider collects your email address and payment details to process the order. We receive your email address and what you bought. We do not receive or store your card number.

How we use it

We use your email address to deliver your book, to answer you if you write to us, and to handle refunds. We do not sell your information.

Website visits

This site counts page visits and button clicks with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter. It does not use cookies and does not build a profile of you.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hello@littlehomebooks.com.
