Who am I

I'm Ruby Mae, sugar. My kitchen is an ordinary one, with a skillet that never leaves the stove and a table that has seen a lot of Sunday dinners. Nothing I cook is fancy. It's cornbread, biscuits, a pot of soup, a pie when somebody is coming over.

Questions

Is this a printed book?

No, sugar. It is a digital book, a PDF. Nothing comes in the mail. You read it on your phone, tablet or computer, or you print it at home.

How do I get it?

Tap the button and pay with your card. Your download is ready right after you pay. If it does not show up, write to ruby@littlehomebooks.com and I will send it to you.

Is it the same as the videos?

A video shows one recipe. This has 100, written down with cups and spoons. The four recipes from my videos are inside too.

Do I need special ingredients or tools?

No special tools. A blender, a loaf pan, a skillet and a baking sheet will carry you through most of the book. A few recipes ask for a waffle iron, popsicle molds or a food processor. The ingredients are at a regular grocery store: almond flour, oat flour, cornmeal, eggs, honey, maple syrup. A few recipes call for psyllium husk powder, tapioca starch or coconut aminos. Most big stores keep those in the baking aisle or the natural foods aisle.

Is it gluten-free?

The recipes are made without wheat flour. They use almond flour, oat flour, cornmeal and coconut flour instead. But they are not tested or certified gluten-free. Oats and other ingredients can be processed alongside wheat. If you must avoid gluten, buy certified gluten-free ingredients and read every label.

Is there any sugar at all?

There is no refined sugar. No white sugar and no brown sugar. The recipes are sweetened with honey, maple syrup, dates and ripe fruit. Those are still sweet, sugar, so this is not a sugar-free book.

Can I print it?

Yes. Print the whole book, or only the recipe you are cooking tonight.
