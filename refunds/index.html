Refunds

Every book has a 30-day money-back guarantee.

How to ask for a refund

Write to hello@littlehomebooks.com within 30 days of your purchase. Use the same email address you used to buy, and tell us which book you bought. You do not need to give a reason.

How you get your money back

The refund goes back to the same card or payment method you used. It can take 5 to 10 business days to show on your statement, depending on your bank.

Did your book not arrive?

Write to the same address and we will send it to you.
